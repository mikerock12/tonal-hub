The locally vendored librespot-core solver uses a reusable SHA-1 prefix state
and stack buffer, and checks its original five-second deadline every 4096
candidates. Protocol and proof verification are unchanged.
The Android host selects the complete Linux desktop OAuth protocol profile:
client ID, client version, User-Agent and client-token platform data agree with
the engine's separate desktop OAuth authorization. Native playback stays Android.
Android-only librespot-core, librespot-audio, librespot-playback and librespot-connect patches also
share the bounded encrypted cache across sessions, publish partial HTTP ranges,
retry interrupted downloads, preserve playback during control reconnection,
and resume prematurely interrupted tracks at the last decoded position.
Corresponding modified source is in android/native/vendor in the local source
archive. These crates retain the original MIT license and copyright notices.
